    Phone notifications

    One-time phone setup
    1. Install the free ntfy app (App Store / Google Play).
    2. In ntfy tap +, subscribe to this topic (server ntfy.sh) and allow notifications:

    The topic name works like a password for your notifications — don't share it.

    🔑 ntfy account token

    Without an account, ntfy.sh limits messages per sending address — and Google's servers are shared by everyone, so the free daily limit is often already used up ("429 limit reached"). A free ntfy.sh account gives you your own limit:

    1. Sign up free at ntfy.sh/signup.
    2. Go to ntfy.sh/account › Access tokens › Create access token (no expiry).
    3. Paste it here (starts with tk_) and press Save below.

    The phone app doesn't need to sign in — it stays subscribed to the same topic.

        ✨ Smart reading (AI)

        With a free Google Gemini key, links are read by AI: a restaurant gets “name, city”, a trip gets the trail or spring name, an activity a short description — and YouTube videos are actually watched. Without a key, simpler rules are used.

        1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
        2. Tap “Create API key” and copy it (starts with AQ. or AIza).
        3. Paste it here and tap Save.

        The key is kept only in your sheet’s script (never in the app). The free tier is plenty for personal use.
